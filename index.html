The resort

A quiet stretch of the
Coromandel, kept as it was

Neythal takes its name from the old Tamil word for the seashore — the landscape of salt pans, screwpine and blue lilies that has defined this coast for two thousand years. We built into it rather than over it: low villas set among existing palms, stone paths that follow the dunes, and a bath house fed by warm mineral water.

Everything here is within a five-minute walk of the water. Nothing here is in a hurry.
